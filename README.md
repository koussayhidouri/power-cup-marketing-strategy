# Power Cup Digital Marketing Strategy

A team academic case study developed for a Digital Marketing course at Tunis Business School. The project applies digital marketing frameworks to More Life Nutrition and its Power Cup product in the Tunisian market. It represents shared team work; it is not an individual consulting engagement or a report of implemented campaign results.

## Project question

How could More Life Nutrition grow awareness and sales for Power Cup while maintaining a healthy-yet-indulgent position in a price-sensitive market?

## What the team developed

- Market and competitor analysis, SWOT, and PESTEL
- Target audiences, personas, and customer journey
- Positioning and a RACE-based digital strategy
- Social media, content, SEO, paid media, and email recommendations
- Proposed objectives and KPIs

The strategy and its targets are recommendations only. The project does not include campaign execution or measured business outcomes. The +30% organic-traffic and +20% repeat-purchase figures are proposed six-month targets, not achieved results.

## Evidence notes

The original report cites estimates that 16% of Tunisians are regular buyers of premium healthy foods and that over 40% of online shoppers spend under 50 TND per month. The ZIP did not include the underlying studies, datasets, or citations, so these figures have not been independently verified and should not be presented as current market facts.

The report says that the website passed LCP and CLS checks but gives no measurements or audit source. See the digital audit notes for that limitation.
