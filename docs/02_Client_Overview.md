# 02. Client Overview

## Executive Summary

More Life Nutrition is a Tunisian healthy-food brand operating in the functional snacks category. Its flagship product, Power Cup, is a protein-rich chocolate cup built around a "clean label" promise: no added sugar, no added oils, and plant-based protein sources. This section profiles the brand and product as the foundation for the strategic analysis that follows.

## Business Context

Tunisia's snack market is dominated by traditional, low-nutrition options and a growing number of imported wellness brands. More Life Nutrition entered this space as a local challenger, betting on a combination of nutritional credibility, convenience, and indulgence to differentiate itself from both ends of the market.

## About the Brand

More Life Nutrition positions itself at the intersection of three trends:

- **Health-conscious consumption**
- **Fitness lifestyle**
- **On-the-go snacking**

The brand's core promise centers on three pillars:

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| No Added Sugar or Oils | A "clean label" promise — every ingredient earns its place |
| Plant-Based Protein Sources | Real nuts, real nutrients, positioned as fuel for an active lifestyle |
| Convenience + Indulgence | Snacking designed to feel good and taste good at the same time |

## About Power Cup

Power Cup is a protein-rich chocolate cup made from ingredients such as nuts and dark chocolate. It is positioned as a healthy, energy-boosting alternative to traditional snacks — designed to answer a specific consumer tension: *"How can I snack without compromising my goals?"*

The product's cup format was identified as a meaningful strength in its own right: portable, visually distinctive, and well-suited to how the target audience already discovers and evaluates food products — through social media.

## Analysis

More Life Nutrition's brand architecture is built around resolving a common emotional conflict for its audience: the desire to snack versus the desire to stay on track with health and fitness goals. Rather than positioning Power Cup purely on nutrition facts, the brand frames it as a lifestyle-compatible indulgence — a positioning choice that shapes every layer of the strategy that follows, from segmentation to content themes.

## Key Findings

- The brand's identity is coherent and differentiated: it does not compete purely on taste or purely on health credentials, but on the combination of both.
- The product's physical format (cup, portable, visual) is inherently compatible with the digital, social-media-driven discovery process of its target audience.
- The brand's positioning statement — "a guilt-free indulgence that fits your lifestyle" — is specific enough to guide marketing decisions, but broad enough to support multiple customer segments (see `08_Target_Audience.md`).

## Recommendations

- Maintain a consistent brand narrative across channels that leads with the *emotional* value proposition ("snack without guilt") before the *functional* one (protein content, no added sugar) — this sequencing is explored further in `13_Marketing_Strategy.md`.
- Use the product's visual strengths deliberately in content production, rather than treating photography/video as an afterthought — detailed in `15_Social_Media_Strategy.md`.
