# 11. Digital Marketing Audit

## Executive Summary

Our team audited More Life Nutrition's existing digital presence, digital micro-environment, and digital macro-environment to establish a clear "where are we now" baseline. The brand is in a digital growth phase: it has a strong product concept and functioning e-commerce infrastructure, but lacks a fully structured digital strategy.

## Business Context

Before recommending new tactics, our team needed an honest audit of what digital assets and channels already existed, how well they were performing structurally, and what gaps stood between the brand's current state and its growth ambitions.

## Analysis

### Current Digital Presence

More Life Nutrition has established a basic but relevant digital presence through its Instagram account and e-commerce website, which currently serve as the brand's main communication and sales channels. Instagram is used to showcase products and build visibility; the website supports direct online purchasing and presents the available product range, including Power Cups, bundles, and gift sets. The website includes practical features such as cart integration, free delivery from 99 TND, and links to social platforms — confirming the brand already operates within a direct-to-consumer digital model.

### Situation Analysis: Where Are We Now?

More Life Nutrition is in a **digital growth phase**. Its products are well adapted to digital marketing because they are visual and attractive, easy to promote through social media, and connected to current health and fitness trends. However, being present online is not sufficient on its own — the brand's digital communication needs to be clearer, more consistent, and more convincing.

### Digital Micro-Environment

**Digital Customers**: likely young, connected, health-conscious consumers interested in fitness, healthy eating, and lifestyle content — generally informed, socially influenced, and inclined to compare products before purchasing.

**Consumer Behavior**: the customer journey is shaped by searching for product information, comparing alternatives, checking reviews or recommendations, and discovering brands through social media — meaning digital channels play a role in **education and trust-building**, not just sales.

**Competitors**: in the digital space, More Life competes not only with other healthy snack brands, but with traditional snacks, imported products, fitness/wellness brands, and lifestyle content competing for the same attention — making the competition **content-based** as well as product-based (see `05_Competitor_Analysis.md`).

**Digital Platforms**: the brand mainly relies on Instagram for awareness and engagement, the website for conversion, and secondary channels — TikTok and Facebook — for additional visibility.

### Digital Macro-Environment

Covered in full in `07_PESTEL.md`. In summary: social and technological conditions favor the brand's digital-first approach, while economic price sensitivity and legal/ethical responsibilities around health claims require ongoing care.

## Key Findings

- More Life Nutrition's digital foundation (website infrastructure, technical SEO compliance, existing social presence) is solid — the gap is in **strategy and consistency**, not infrastructure.
- Digital channels serve a dual role for this brand: sales conversion *and* consumer education, given low baseline category awareness.
- The competitive set in digital spaces is broader than the physical competitive set, since the brand is also competing for attention against unrelated lifestyle content.

## Recommendations

- Build on the existing technical SEO strengths by focusing effort on content and acquisition strategy — the opportunity lies in scaling visibility, not fixing infrastructure (see `16_SEO_Strategy.md`).
- Formalize a content and engagement strategy for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook individually, rather than treating "social media" as a single undifferentiated channel (see `15_Social_Media_Strategy.md`).
- Use the digital customer journey (`10_Customer_Journey.md`) to ensure every stage — not just awareness — has a corresponding digital touchpoint.
