# 15. Social Media Strategy

## Executive Summary

Our team developed a platform-specific social media strategy, assigning each channel a distinct role within the RACE framework rather than duplicating the same content across platforms. TikTok drives discovery, Instagram builds premium brand storytelling, and Facebook sustains community and retention.

## Business Context

The digital audit (`11_Digital_Marketing_Audit.md`) identified inconsistent, under-strategized use of social channels as a core weakness. This section defines a clear division of labor across platforms to correct that.

## Analysis

### TikTok — The Discovery Engine

**Objective:** Generate rapid brand awareness through relatable, authentic short-form content.
**RACE Contribution:** Reach

*Execution:* "The Realistic Wellness Journey" content format — busy student routines, campus snack swaps, gym-to-class transitions — paired with "Man on the Street" taste-test videos at Tunisian universities to build authenticity and relatability.

### Instagram — Premium Brand Storytelling

**Objective:** Build a premium visual identity that justifies the brand's positioning.
**RACE Contribution:** Act / Convert

*Execution:* "Inside the Cup" ingredient storytelling using macro photography, real textures, hand-drawn ingredient sketches, and cinematic transitions — visually proving quality while making healthy snacks feel indulgent and desirable. Complemented by Tunisian lifestyle integration (e.g., summer-friendly snack positioning, energy-focused wellness content).

### Facebook — Community & Retention

**Objective:** Strengthen customer loyalty through community interaction.
**RACE Contribution:** Engage

*Execution:* "The Mystery Flavor Poll" — interactive community voting campaigns where users choose future flavors inspired by Tunisian ingredients (e.g., Zgougou, Deglet Nour, walnuts), increasing emotional investment in the brand while encouraging repeat interaction.

### Real-Life Integration Campaign: "Day in My Life"

**Target:** Students and active consumers.
**Objective:** Reduce price resistance by reframing the product as an accessible daily upgrade.
**RACE Contribution:** Convert

This campaign follows a persona (Yasmine, see `09_Customer_Personas.md`) sharing her experience on social media, tagging the brand, and gradually becoming a micro-advocate — directly modeling the Loyalty stage behavior documented in the customer journey.

## Key Findings

- Platform roles are intentionally differentiated: TikTok is optimized for cold-audience discovery, Instagram for brand-building and conversion-adjacent storytelling, and Facebook for retention and community — avoiding a one-size-fits-all social strategy.
- Cultural specificity (Tunisian ingredient references, local university settings) recurs across platforms as the brand's primary differentiation lever in social content, consistent with the competitive analysis.
- Interactive formats (polls, "Man on the Street" content) are used deliberately to convert passive viewers into active participants, supporting the Engagement objective defined in `12_Marketing_Objectives.md`.

## Recommendations

- Maintain platform-specific content rather than cross-posting identical content, since each platform is assigned a distinct funnel role.
- Track engagement rate performance against the 5% target (Objective 4, `12_Marketing_Objectives.md`) separately by platform, since TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook are likely to perform differently given their differing RACE roles.
- Pair the weekly content calendar (`14_Content_Strategy.md`) with this platform strategy to ensure execution stays consistent over time rather than being campaign-dependent.
