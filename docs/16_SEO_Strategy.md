# 16. SEO Strategy

## Executive Summary

Our team's SEO strategy spans technical foundations, on-page content, and off-page authority-building, with the goal of positioning More Life Nutrition as a top-ranking healthy snack brand for high-intent Tunisian searches. The website already meets core technical benchmarks; the opportunity lies in content and acquisition strategy.

## Business Context

SEO was identified as a primary lever for the Reach stage of the RACE framework, particularly valuable given the brand's limited paid media budget relative to larger competitors — organic discoverability offers a more sustainable long-term acquisition channel.

## Analysis

### Technical SEO Foundation

**Objective:** Maintain a technically sound website and provide a usable mobile experience for Tunisian visitors.
**RACE Contribution:** Reach

### On-Page SEO & Content Optimization

**Objective:** Position More Life as a top-ranking healthy snack brand for high-intent Tunisian searches.
**RACE Contribution:** Reach

**1. Bilingual SEO Strategy** — Implement a French-English (and Arabic, per the digital objectives) keyword structure using hreflang tags to capture multilingual search behavior, e.g., "Healthy snacks Tunisia" / "Snacks sains Tunisie."

**2. Semantic Content Expansion** — Avoid thin product pages by integrating nutritional explanations, ingredient storytelling, and health-focused semantic keywords (e.g., low glycemic index, natural sweeteners, energy-boosting snacks).

**3. E-E-A-T & YMYL Optimization** — As a health-adjacent brand, strengthen credibility through expert-backed nutrition sections, ingredient transparency, and specialist-written health explanations, increasing trust while complying with Google's standards for health content.

**4. Product Page Content** — Give each product page clear, useful information about ingredients, product experience, and the brand. The project did not validate a minimum word count or a ranking effect from page length.

**5. Topical Authority** — Create a pillar page (e.g., "The Ultimate Guide to Healthy Snacking in Tunisia") linked to supporting content clusters such as "5 Best Post-Workout Snacks in Tunis."

**6. Long-Tail Keyword Approach** — Capture underserved search demand by targeting specific, real user search intent, such as "healthy snacks for kids Tunisia," "sugar-free energy bars Tunis," "protein snacks Tunisia price," and "healthy dessert alternatives Tunisia."

### Off-Page SEO & Brand Authority

**Objective:** Increase domain authority and brand trust through high-quality external visibility.
**RACE Contribution:** Reach

**1. Nutritionist Partnership Strategy** — Launch a co-branded initiative (e.g., "Power Morning Challenge") in collaboration with Tunisian nutritionists and wellness creators, generating guest blog articles, social reposts, and backlinks from trusted wellness sources.

**2. Local SEO Optimization** — Create and optimize a Google Business Profile in Tunis to appear in local "healthy food near me" searches, even as a primarily e-commerce brand.

**3. Backlink Profile** — Partner with Tunisian fitness influencers and wellness bloggers for earned media links, verifying the brand's credibility through local vouching and physical presence.

## Key Findings

- The brand's technical SEO foundation is not a bottleneck — the growth opportunity is concentrated in content depth, keyword targeting, and off-page authority.
- Bilingual (and multilingual) keyword strategy is essential given Tunisia's mixed French/Arabic search behavior — a single-language approach would leave significant search demand uncaptured.
- Trust-building content (E-E-A-T, nutritionist partnerships) serves a dual purpose: improving search rankings for health-related content *and* directly supporting the brand's Social Proof marketing pillar.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize the 300-word content rule and semantic expansion across all product pages before investing further in link-building, since content depth compounds the value of any backlinks earned.
- Launch the nutritionist partnership initiative early, since it serves both SEO authority-building and the broader trust/credibility objectives defined in `13_Marketing_Strategy.md`.
- Track organic traffic growth against the final strategic target of +30% organic traffic within six months (see `22_Final_Recommendations.md`).
