# 09. Customer Personas

## Executive Summary

Our team developed two representative personas — Yasmine and Karim — to ground the target audience definition in concrete behavior. These personas are built from the digital touchpoints and decision drivers documented in the customer journey work, rather than as fully independent demographic profiles.

## Business Context

Personas help translate the broader target audience definition (`08_Target_Audience.md`) into decisions about content, channels, and messaging. Rather than inventing detailed biographical profiles, our team kept these personas tied closely to observed behavior at each stage of the customer journey, to preserve accuracy to the source research.

## Analysis

### Persona 1: Yasmine

Represents the **primary target** — a young, urban, social-media-active consumer beginning or building a fitness routine.

| Journey Stage | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Discovers More Life Nutrition through an Instagram reel showcasing a beginner-friendly fitness transformation |
| Consideration | Visits the Instagram page, explores product descriptions, and is reassured by the brand's local Tunisian positioning and accessible pricing |
| Purchase | Orders a starter protein bundle online, motivated by a first-time purchase discount and her fitness goals |
| Loyalty | Shares her experience on social media, tags the brand, and gradually becomes a micro-advocate within her network |

### Persona 2: Karim

Represents the **secondary target** — a consumer motivated more by convenience and practicality than by fitness identity, and who evaluates purchases more cautiously.

| Journey Stage | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Encounters a targeted Facebook advertisement after searching for "healthy snacks Tunisia" |
| Consideration | Reviews ingredients, compares the product with his usual snacks, and looks for social proof such as testimonials and reviews |
| Purchase | Chooses a low-commitment sample pack, in-store or via delivery, to test the product without risk |
| Loyalty | Repurchases his preferred flavors and may recommend the product to colleagues or peers |

## Key Findings

- Yasmine's journey is driven primarily by **aspiration and social validation** — she responds to lifestyle content and community proof.
- Karim's journey is driven primarily by **risk reduction and evidence** — he responds to ingredient transparency, comparison, and low-commitment trial options.
- Both personas ultimately convert into advocates, but through different mechanisms — social sharing for Yasmine, word-of-mouth recommendation for Karim — which has direct implications for retention tactics (see `18_Email_Marketing.md` and `15_Social_Media_Strategy.md`).

## Recommendations

- Design at least two distinct content tracks: aspirational/lifestyle content for Yasmine-type consumers, and evidence/trust-building content for Karim-type consumers.
- Offer low-commitment entry points (sample packs, starter bundles) for both personas, since both journeys show purchase hesitation being resolved through reduced-risk offers rather than full-price commitment.

> **Note:** The source material does not include full demographic persona details (age, occupation, income, quotes, or photos) beyond the journey behaviors documented above. This section reflects only what was defined in the original customer journey research.
