# 12. Marketing Objectives

## Executive Summary

Our team defined six digital marketing objectives, each measurable, time-bound, and directly linked to a specific finding from the SWOT and digital audit. Together, they provide More Life Nutrition with a structured roadmap to grow its online presence, convert its target audience into loyal customers, and strengthen its brand positioning in a competitive, price-sensitive market.

## Business Context

Rather than proposing broad, unmeasurable ambitions, our team anchored each objective to a specific KPI, timeline, and strategic rationale — ensuring the objectives could be tracked and evaluated, and that each one traces back to a documented gap in the brand's current position.

## Analysis

### Objective 1: Increase Brand Awareness on Social Media

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Grow social media presence (Instagram & TikTok) by 30% within 6 months |
| KPI | Number of followers, reach per post, impressions |
| Timeline | 6 months |
| Strategy | Consistent content posting (4–5x/week) using lifestyle visuals and short-form video; partnerships with Tunisian micro-influencers in fitness and wellness; targeted paid ads aimed at urban audiences aged 18–30 |

*Rationale:* Directly addresses the weak digital presence identified in the SWOT and supports the Lifestyle Branding marketing pillar.

### Objective 2: Drive Traffic to the Online Store

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Increase monthly website/online store visits by 40% within 6 months |
| KPI | Monthly website sessions, click-through rate (CTR) from social media |
| Timeline | 6 months |
| Strategy | Direct purchase links in Instagram and TikTok bios; product page optimization with relevant French and Arabic keywords (e.g., "snack sain Tunisie," "cup protéiné"); Google Ads campaigns for local searches |

*Rationale:* Aligned with the e-commerce growth opportunity identified in market research and the distribution strategy that prioritizes Instagram as the primary discovery channel.

### Objective 3: Boost Online Sales and Conversions

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Achieve a 15% increase in online orders within 6 months |
| KPI | Number of online orders, conversion rate, average order value |
| Timeline | 6 months |
| Strategy | Limited-time promotions on Instagram Stories and Facebook Ads; retargeting of visitors who did not purchase; first-order discounts for new customers |

*Rationale:* Responds directly to the price-sensitivity threat identified in the SWOT, through the Accessibility & Value Perception marketing pillar.

### Objective 4: Build Community Engagement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Reach an average engagement rate of 5% on Instagram and TikTok within 3 months |
| KPI | Likes, comments, shares, saves, story interactions per post |
| Timeline | 3 months |
| Strategy | User-generated content (UGC) campaign encouraging customers to share their "Power Cup moment"; polls, Q&As, and fitness challenges; real customer testimonials |

*Rationale:* Directly supports the Social Proof & Influence marketing pillar — authentic content strengthens trust for a product claiming both health and taste benefits.

### Objective 5: Establish B2B Digital Partnerships

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Secure 5 active digital partnerships with gyms or fitness studios within 6 months |
| KPI | Number of partnerships, referral code uses, partner-generated content |
| Timeline | 6 months |
| Strategy | Collaboration with CrossFit boxes, gyms, and fitness coaches through affiliate codes and co-created content; exclusive discounts for their community members |

*Rationale:* Activates the B2B digital partnership opportunity from the SWOT and expands distribution reach beyond niche "Bio" shops.

### Objective 6: Grow and Retain a Loyal Customer Base

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Objective | Build an email and/or WhatsApp subscriber list of 500+ contacts, maintaining a 25% open/read rate within 6 months |
| KPI | Subscriber count, open rate, repeat purchase rate |
| Timeline | 6 months |
| Strategy | First-purchase discount in exchange for newsletter sign-up; bi-weekly nutrition tips, product launches, and exclusive offers; WhatsApp broadcast lists for direct communication |

*Rationale:* Supports long-term customer retention and addresses the limited-product-range weakness by keeping customers informed of new releases and variations.

## Key Findings

- Every objective is tied to a specific KPI and timeline, avoiding vague or unmeasurable ambitions.
- Objectives collectively span the full funnel — from awareness (Objective 1) through retention (Objective 6) — rather than concentrating solely on top-of-funnel growth.
- Two objectives (4 and 6) carry direct implications for long-term brand equity, not just short-term conversion, reflecting the brand's need to build trust in a price-sensitive market.

## Recommendations

- Track all six objectives against their stated KPIs on the stated timelines, and treat the 3-month engagement objective as an early checkpoint for the overall strategy's traction.
- Sequence execution so that Objective 1 (awareness) and Objective 4 (engagement) are prioritized early, since later objectives (traffic, conversion, retention) depend on there being an audience to convert in the first place.
