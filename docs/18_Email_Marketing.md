# 18. Email Marketing & CRM

## Executive Summary

Our team's retention strategy centers on building a combined email and WhatsApp subscriber base, using both channels for direct, personal communication that supports repeat purchase and long-term loyalty — the Engage stage of the RACE framework.

## Business Context

The SWOT analysis identified irregular purchase frequency and limited product range as risks to sustained revenue. A direct communication channel — rather than relying solely on social media reach, which is subject to algorithmic visibility — was identified as a necessary complement to social strategy.

## Analysis

### Objective

Build an email and/or WhatsApp subscriber list of 500+ contacts, maintaining a 25% open/read rate within 6 months (see `12_Marketing_Objectives.md`, Objective 6).

### Execution Strategy

**List Building** — Offer a first-purchase discount in exchange for newsletter sign-up, converting one-time visitors into an owned communication channel rather than relying solely on repeat organic visits.

**Email Content** — Bi-weekly nutrition tips, product launches, and exclusive offers, positioning the newsletter as a value-adding resource rather than a purely promotional channel — consistent with the Education marketing pillar.

**WhatsApp Broadcast** — Used for direct, personal communication with existing customers, offering a higher-intimacy channel appropriate for a market where WhatsApp is a primary daily communication tool.

**Loyalty & Retention Systems** — Monthly subscription and loyalty reward concepts (see the "Moula Box" subscription model referenced in the presentation materials) are positioned as tools to convert one-time buyers into recurring customers, directly supporting customer lifetime value.

**Exclusive Access** — Early access to new launches and exclusive offers for subscribers, designed to create anticipation and reward loyal customers specifically, differentiating the subscriber experience from the general public-facing social content.

## Key Findings

- Email and WhatsApp are treated as complementary, not redundant — email supports structured, value-driven content, while WhatsApp supports immediacy and personal connection.
- The subscription/loyalty model is designed to directly address the "limited product range" weakness identified in the SWOT, by keeping customers engaged with new releases and variations over time rather than relying on assortment breadth.
- Retention strategy is explicitly linked to advocacy: engaged subscribers are the audience most likely to generate the word-of-mouth recommendations documented in the customer journey's Loyalty stage.

## Recommendations

- Launch list-building (the first-purchase discount incentive) early and in parallel with awareness campaigns, since subscriber acquisition depends on having site traffic to convert in the first place.
- Monitor open/read rates against the 25% target closely in the first weeks, since email/WhatsApp engagement typically declines from an initial peak — early data will indicate whether content cadence needs adjustment.
- Treat the subscription/loyalty concept as a mid-term initiative to pilot once the core list-building and content cadence are established, rather than a day-one requirement.
