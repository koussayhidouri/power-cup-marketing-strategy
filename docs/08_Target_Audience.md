# 08. Target Audience

## Executive Summary

Our team defined a primary and secondary target audience for Power Cup, unified by a shared emotional tension: the desire to snack without compromising health or fitness goals. This section defines who the brand is speaking to and why.

## Business Context

Given the brand's limited marketing resources relative to larger competitors, precise audience definition was essential — the strategy needed to concentrate effort on the segments most likely to convert and become repeat customers, rather than pursuing a broad, undifferentiated audience.

## Analysis

### Primary Target

- Young adults (18–30)
- Gym-goers and fitness beginners
- Urban, social-media-active consumers

### Secondary Target

- People trying to eat healthier, not necessarily athletes
- Students and young professionals

### The Unifying Insight

Across both segments, our team identified a shared psychological driver:

> Target **people who feel guilty when they snack.**

This insight — rather than demographic profile alone — is what shapes the brand's positioning: *"A guilt-free indulgence that fits your lifestyle."* The brand is designed to live in the space between fitness discipline and everyday cravings, rather than exclusively serving committed athletes or purely indulgence-driven snackers.

### Digital Behavior of the Target Audience

Our team's digital context analysis (see `11_Digital_Marketing_Audit.md`) found that More Life's likely digital audience is composed of young, connected, health-conscious consumers who are generally informed, socially influenced, and inclined to compare products before purchasing — behavior that reinforces the importance of trust-building content, not just visibility.

## Key Findings

- The primary and secondary segments are distinguished less by demographics and more by *motivation* — fitness-driven versus wellness-curious.
- Both segments are digitally active and rely on social platforms for product discovery, comparison, and validation, making digital channels the most efficient way to reach them.
- The "guilt" insight is the throughline that should inform tone and messaging across every channel, not just top-of-funnel advertising.

## Recommendations

- Build messaging architecture around the guilt/indulgence tension rather than purely nutritional facts — detailed further in `13_Marketing_Strategy.md`.
- Differentiate content slightly by segment: fitness-performance framing for the primary target, ease/balance framing for the secondary target, while keeping the core positioning consistent.
- Use the customer personas in `09_Customer_Personas.md` and journey mapping in `10_Customer_Journey.md` to translate this audience definition into concrete content and campaign decisions.
