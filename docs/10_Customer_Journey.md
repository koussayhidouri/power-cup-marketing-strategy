# 10. Customer Journey

## Executive Summary

Our team mapped the customer journey across four stages — Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, and Loyalty — to understand how digital touchpoints influence the path from discovery to advocacy. This mapping directly informs the channel and content decisions throughout the rest of the strategy.

## Business Context

Understanding the customer journey was essential to ensuring that digital marketing efforts support the customer at every stage of the decision process, rather than concentrating exclusively on awareness or exclusively on conversion.

## Analysis

### 1. Awareness Stage

Both personas are exposed to the brand through different digital touchpoints — Yasmine through an Instagram reel showcasing a fitness transformation, and Karim through a targeted Facebook ad triggered by a "healthy snacks Tunisia" search. This stage is driven primarily by **social media visibility and targeted digital advertising**.

### 2. Consideration Stage

Both personas actively evaluate the brand and product before committing. Yasmine is reassured by the brand's local Tunisian positioning and accessible pricing; Karim reviews ingredients, compares the product to his usual snacks, and looks for social proof such as testimonials and reviews. This stage is primarily influenced by **trust, clarity of information, and perceived value**.

### 3. Purchase Stage

The purchase decision is reached through different motivations. Yasmine orders a starter protein bundle, encouraged by a first-time purchase discount and her fitness goals. Karim opts for a low-commitment sample pack, in-store or via delivery, to test the product without risk. This stage is driven by **accessibility, pricing incentives, and reduced friction**.

### 4. Loyalty Stage

After the initial purchase, both personas transition into retention behaviors. Yasmine shares her experience on social media, tags the brand, and gradually becomes a micro-advocate. Karim repurchases his preferred flavors and may recommend the product to colleagues or peers. Retention is supported through **loyalty incentives, consistent content engagement, and sustained product satisfaction**.

### Journey-to-RACE Alignment

This four-stage journey maps directly onto the RACE framework used to structure the full digital strategy (`13_Marketing_Strategy.md`):

| Journey Stage | RACE Stage | Primary Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach | Visibility and discovery |
| Consideration | Act | Engagement and trust-building |
| Purchase | Convert | Sales and conversion |
| Loyalty | Engage | Retention and advocacy |

## Key Findings

- The journey is not linear in intensity — the Consideration stage carries the most weight, since both personas actively seek validation before purchasing, rather than converting immediately from awareness.
- Trust-building content (ingredient transparency, testimonials, social proof) is a recurring need across both personas at the Consideration stage, regardless of what triggered initial awareness.
- Loyalty is achieved through different mechanisms per persona — social sharing versus quiet repurchase and word-of-mouth — meaning retention tactics should not assume uniform advocacy behavior.

## Recommendations

- Invest specifically in Consideration-stage content (ingredient storytelling, testimonials, comparison-friendly product information), since this is where both personas' journeys converge on the same needs.
- Structure loyalty programs to reward both visible advocacy (shares, tags, UGC) and quiet repurchase behavior, rather than only incentivizing public promotion.
- Use this journey map as the direct blueprint for the RACE-based execution strategy detailed in `13_Marketing_Strategy.md` through `18_Email_Marketing.md`.
