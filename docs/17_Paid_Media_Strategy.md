# 17. Paid Media Strategy

## Executive Summary

Our team designed three paid media campaign concepts, each targeting a distinct audience moment and mapped to the Convert stage of the RACE framework. All three are built to reduce purchase hesitation in a price-sensitive market rather than compete on price alone.

## Business Context

Given the price sensitivity documented in `04_Market_Research.md` and `06_SWOT.md`, paid media was designed to focus less on discount-driven advertising and more on reframing perceived value — positioning Power Cup as an accessible upgrade rather than a discretionary luxury.

## Analysis

### Campaign 1: "Desk-Side Hack"

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target | Young professionals and office workers |
| Objective | Position More Life as a practical solution for afternoon energy crashes |
| RACE Contribution | Convert |
| Execution | Visual transition: fatigued employee during the afternoon slump → product consumption moment → return to focus and productivity |
| Platforms | Instagram Stories, Reels |
| CTA | "Upgrade Your Office Snack." |

### Campaign 2: "Inside the Cup"

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target | Students and professionals |
| Objective | Build premium perception while proving product quality visually |
| RACE Contribution | Act / Convert |
| Execution | High-production macro visuals: chocolate cracking open, ingredients emerging dynamically, hand-drawn ingredient overlays, cinematic slow-motion transitions |
| Platforms | TikTok Top View, Instagram Feed |
| CTA | "Taste the Revolution." |

### Campaign 3: "Day in My Life"

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target | Students and active consumers |
| Objective | Reduce price resistance by reframing the product as an accessible daily upgrade |
| RACE Contribution | Convert |
| Execution | Minimal, clean visuals paired with quick educational messaging on More Life snack value |
| Platforms | Instagram Stories, Reels |
| CTA | "Start Your Journey for Less." |

## Key Findings

- All three campaigns target a specific *moment* (afternoon slump, product-quality proof point, daily routine) rather than a generic product ad, aligning with the Lifestyle Branding pillar's emphasis on real-life context.
- Two of the three campaigns (Desk-Side Hack, Day in My Life) are explicitly designed to counter price resistance through reframing rather than discounting — consistent with the Accessibility & Value Perception pillar.
- "Inside the Cup" is the only campaign concept that leads primarily with product-quality proof rather than lifestyle context, positioning it as the credibility anchor across the paid media set.

## Recommendations

- Sequence campaigns to match audience readiness: use "Inside the Cup" for cold or skeptical audiences needing quality proof, and "Desk-Side Hack" / "Day in My Life" for warmer audiences closer to a purchase decision.
- Pair paid campaigns with the retargeting and first-order discount tactics defined in `12_Marketing_Objectives.md` (Objective 3) to capture visitors who engage with an ad but don't convert immediately.
- Track each campaign's contribution to the +15% online order growth objective, segmenting performance by CTA and platform to identify which campaign concept converts most efficiently.

> **Note:** The source material does not include specific ad spend, budget allocation, or projected ROAS/CPA figures for these campaigns. This section documents campaign concepts and targeting logic only.
