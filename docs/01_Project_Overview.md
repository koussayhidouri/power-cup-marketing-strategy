# 01. Project Overview

## Executive Summary

More Life Nutrition, a Tunisian healthy-snack brand, engaged our team to develop a comprehensive digital marketing strategy for its flagship product, **Power Cup** — a protein-rich chocolate cup positioned as a guilt-free alternative to traditional snacks. Our team analyzed the brand's internal capabilities, external market conditions, digital presence, and customer behavior to answer one central strategic question, and to propose a structured, measurable path to growth.

## Business Context

More Life Nutrition operates at the intersection of three converging consumer trends in Tunisia: health-conscious eating, fitness culture, and on-the-go snacking. Power Cup was designed to serve consumers who want to snack without the trade-offs typically associated with indulgent food — added sugar, processed oils, or a lack of nutritional value.

The brand had already built the foundations of a digital presence (an Instagram account and an e-commerce website) and a clear product concept, but lacked a structured strategy to translate that foundation into sustained brand awareness, engagement, and sales.

## The Strategic Question

Our engagement was framed around a single guiding question:

> **How can More Life Nutrition scale its brand awareness and sales in Tunisia while maintaining a strong "healthy yet indulgent" positioning in a price-sensitive market?**

This question anchors every subsequent section of this case study — from the situational analysis through to the final recommendations.

## Project Objectives

Our team structured the engagement around four core analytical and strategic goals:

| Objective | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Evaluate internal and external factors** | Conduct a full SWOT and PESTEL analysis to understand the brand's position |
| **Propose a targeted marketing strategy** | Define positioning, segments, value proposition, and marketing pillars |
| **Assess the digital ecosystem and presence** | Audit current digital channels and identify structural gaps |
| **Define clear marketing and digital objectives** | Translate strategy into measurable, time-bound digital objectives |

## Methodology

Our approach followed a standard strategic marketing methodology, moving from diagnosis to strategy to execution:

1. **Situation analysis** — internal capability review (SWOT) and external environment scan (PESTEL)
2. **Strategic positioning** — defining target segments, value proposition, and core marketing pillars
3. **Digital context analysis** — auditing existing digital presence, digital micro- and macro-environment, and customer journey
4. **Strategy design** — applying the RACE framework (Reach, Act, Convert, Engage) to structure the full digital marketing plan
5. **Execution planning** — translating strategy into channel-specific tactics (SEO, social media, paid media, email/CRM)
6. **Objective-setting** — defining KPI-backed digital marketing objectives across a 3–6 month horizon

## Key Findings (Preview)

- Power Cup's product characteristics — visual appeal, portability, and alignment with wellness trends — make it naturally well-suited to digital marketing.
- The brand's core constraint is not product-market fit, but **digital structure**: weak brand awareness, inconsistent digital presence, and no systematic approach to converting interest into repeat purchases.
- Tunisia's price-sensitive market context means that **value perception**, not price competition alone, is the more sustainable lever for growth.

Each of these findings is explored in full detail in the sections that follow.
