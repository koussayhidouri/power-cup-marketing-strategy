# 20. KPIs

## Executive Summary

Our team defined a specific set of KPIs for each of the six digital marketing objectives, ensuring every strategic goal has a measurable way to track progress. This section consolidates those KPIs and explains what each one measures in the context of this strategy.

## Business Context

Measurability was a core requirement of this engagement — every objective in `12_Marketing_Objectives.md` needed a corresponding KPI so that progress could be evaluated against a defined timeline rather than assessed subjectively.

## Analysis

### KPIs by Objective

| Objective | KPIs Used |
|---|---|
| 1. Brand Awareness | Number of followers, reach per post, impressions |
| 2. Website Traffic | Monthly website sessions, click-through rate (CTR) from social media |
| 3. Sales & Conversion | Number of online orders, conversion rate, average order value |
| 4. Community Engagement | Likes, comments, shares, saves, story interactions per post |
| 5. B2B Partnerships | Number of partnerships, referral code uses, partner-generated content |
| 6. Loyalty & Retention | Subscriber count, open rate, repeat purchase rate |

### KPI Definitions

**Reach / Impressions** — The number of unique users exposed to content (reach) versus the total number of times content was displayed, including repeat views (impressions). Used to track Objective 1's awareness growth.

**Followers** — Total audience size on Instagram and TikTok, tracked against the 30% growth target over 6 months.

**Website Sessions** — Total visits to the website, used to measure whether social and search efforts are successfully driving traffic (Objective 2).

**Click-Through Rate (CTR)** — The percentage of people who click through from a social post or ad to the website, measuring the effectiveness of the link between awareness and consideration.

**Conversion Rate** — The percentage of website visitors who complete a purchase, used to evaluate how well the site converts existing traffic (Objective 3).

**Average Order Value (AOV)** — The average amount spent per order, relevant given the price-sensitive market context documented in `04_Market_Research.md`.

**Engagement Rate** — Likes, comments, shares, saves, and story interactions relative to reach or followers, used to measure how actively the audience is participating rather than just viewing (Objective 4).

**Referral Code Uses** — Tracks how many purchases originate from B2B partner codes (gyms, CrossFit boxes), directly measuring the performance of Objective 5's partnership strategy.

**Subscriber Count & Open Rate** — Track the size and engagement level of the email/WhatsApp list built under Objective 6, with a 25% open/read rate as the defined benchmark.

**Repeat Purchase Rate** — Tracks the percentage of customers making more than one purchase, a critical measure given the "irregular purchase frequency" threat identified in the SWOT.

## Key Findings

- KPIs are distributed across the full funnel — awareness (followers, reach), engagement (interactions), conversion (orders, conversion rate), and retention (subscriber count, repeat purchase rate) — avoiding an over-index on top-of-funnel vanity metrics alone.
- Several KPIs (CTR, conversion rate, repeat purchase rate) directly connect two adjacent funnel stages, allowing the brand to diagnose *where* in the journey performance is weakest, not just whether overall growth occurred.
- No baseline (starting) figures for these KPIs are provided in the source material — targets are expressed as percentage growth (e.g., +30% reach), which assumes access to current performance data not included in this project's documentation.

## Recommendations

- Establish a simple KPI dashboard tracking all metrics above against their timelines (3 or 6 months per objective), reviewed at least monthly.
- Treat the 3-month engagement KPI checkpoint (Objective 4) as an early diagnostic for the broader 6-month strategy, since engagement issues discovered early can be corrected before conversion and retention objectives come due.

> **Note:** Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), specific revenue figures, and a numeric Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) target are not included in the source material and are therefore not reported here, consistent with this project's policy against fabricating figures.
