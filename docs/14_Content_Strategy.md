# 14. Content Strategy

## Executive Summary

Our team's content strategy translates the four marketing pillars (`13_Marketing_Strategy.md`) into recurring, structured content themes across platforms, anchored by a weekly content calendar that ties each piece of content to a specific RACE stage.

## Business Context

Consistency was identified as a key gap in More Life Nutrition's existing digital presence (see `11_Digital_Marketing_Audit.md`). A structured content strategy addresses this directly by giving the brand a repeatable content system rather than ad hoc posting.

## Analysis

### Core Content Themes

**"Inside the Cup" — Ingredient Storytelling**
Macro photography, real textures, hand-drawn ingredient sketches, and cinematic transitions used to visually prove product quality and make healthy snacking feel indulgent and desirable.

**"The Realistic Wellness Journey"**
Content centered on the reality of maintaining healthy habits in Tunisia — busy student routines, campus snack swaps, gym-to-class transitions — designed for relatability over aspiration alone.

**Tunisian Lifestyle Integration**
Culturally relevant positioning such as summer-friendly snacking occasions and energy-focused wellness content, reinforcing the brand's local identity as a competitive differentiator (see `05_Competitor_Analysis.md`).

**Educational & Trust-Building Content**
Nutrition benefit explanations and ingredient storytelling designed to close the category-education gap identified in the Marketing Strategy's Education pillar.

**User-Generated & Community Content**
UGC campaigns inviting customers to share their "Power Cup moment," alongside interactive formats like polls and Q&As that convert passive followers into active participants.

### Weekly Content Calendar

| Day | Platform | Objective | RACE Stage | Content Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | TikTok / Reels | Awareness | Reach | "Gym Bag Essentials for 10DT" |
| Wednesday | Instagram | Engagement | Act | 3D ingredient motion content |
| Thursday | Stories | Community interaction | Engage | Flavor poll & waitlist |
| Friday | Facebook / Instagram | Conversion | Convert | Weekend bundle promotion |

## Key Findings

- Content themes are deliberately distributed across RACE stages within a single week, rather than concentrating all content on awareness alone.
- The "Inside the Cup" theme directly operationalizes the Education and Lifestyle Branding pillars by making ingredient information visually engaging rather than clinical.
- Cultural specificity (Tunisian lifestyle integration) recurs as a theme because it was identified as a durable competitive advantage in `05_Competitor_Analysis.md`.

## Recommendations

- Maintain the weekly cadence structure as a minimum viable content system, then scale frequency once the 4–5x/week posting target from `12_Marketing_Objectives.md` (Objective 1) is consistently met.
- Rotate UGC and educational content regularly to avoid the brand's messaging becoming overly promotional, which risks undermining the trust-building goals of the Social Proof pillar.
- Coordinate content themes across platforms so that "Inside the Cup" storytelling on Instagram is echoed (in shorter form) on TikTok, reinforcing message consistency across channels — detailed further in `15_Social_Media_Strategy.md`.
