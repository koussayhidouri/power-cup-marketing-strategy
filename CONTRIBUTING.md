# Contributing

This repository documents a completed academic consulting project — a digital marketing strategy developed for **More Life Nutrition's Power Cup**, produced as part of a Digital Marketing course at Tunis Business School.

## Project Status

This is a **closed project**. It reflects a specific engagement completed by our team at a fixed point in time and is not an actively maintained open-source project. As such:

- External pull requests are not being accepted.
- The strategy, analysis, and recommendations reflect the scope and information available during the original engagement and are not being updated to reflect market changes since.

## About the Work

The content in this repository was developed collaboratively by our team. All strategic analysis, recommendations, and documentation reflect a shared effort rather than individual contributions.

## Questions or Feedback

If you have questions about the methodology, frameworks used, or findings, feel free to open an issue — we're happy to discuss the thinking behind the work, even though the project itself isn't open for direct contributions.
