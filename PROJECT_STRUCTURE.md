Sections 19 (implementation roadmap) and 21 (risk assessment) are omitted because the original project did not provide a resourced roadmap or formal risk register. The repository does not invent those deliverables.

## Reading guide

- **Overview:** Start with `README.md`, then `docs/01_Project_Overview.md`.
- **Analysis:** Read `docs/04_Market_Research.md` through `docs/11_Digital_Marketing_Audit.md`.
- **Strategy and execution:** Read `docs/12_Marketing_Objectives.md` through `docs/18_Email_Marketing.md`.
- **Measurement and reflection:** Read `docs/20_KPIs.md`, `docs/22_Final_Recommendations.md`, and `docs/23_Lessons_Learned.md`.

The market estimates and website audit statements are summarized from the original report. The report package does not include the underlying citations or measurement records; see the evidence notes in `README.md` and the digital audit.
